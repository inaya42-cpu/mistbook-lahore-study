# Mistbook Lahore Study

Snap mistakes, spot patterns, and revise smarter for lahore board punjab(class 11) with mistbook.

Doltbook is your smarter punjab lahore board mistake book.

Snap a question, say what went wrong, and let mistbook organize the lesson for you. No long typing. No messy notebook maintenance. Just capture the mistake while it is fresh, then return when you are ready to revise.

With mistbook, you can:

- Capture questions with a photo, voice note, or text

- Keep mistakes organized by subject, chapter, and topic

- See patterns in the errors you repeat

- Get clear reminders for what to remember next time

- Review your saved mistakes when you need them

Your job is simple: log the mistake.

Mistbook helps turn it into a better revision habit.
